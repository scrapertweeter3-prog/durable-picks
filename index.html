DurablePicks

DurablePicks is my buy-it-for-life review site, and the method behind every writeup is cost per decade. A $30 pair of socks that lasts ten years is cheaper than a $14 pair replaced annually, and the sock comparison runs that arithmetic on Darn Tough, Smartwool, and Icebreaker with each lifetime guarantee in the denominator.

Serviceability is the durability feature

The espresso machine roundup ranks boilers by metallurgy and checks for an E61 group, because a machine you can service stays in service while sealed units head to the landfill. Repairability scoring runs through the other categories too.

Ranked by how they fail

Work pants are ranked by how they fail, and the mug roundup grades ceramics on how they die, MiiR among them. The duffel comparison prices Saddleback, Filson, and Clegg bags over a 30-year horizon. Paint sprayers get the same treatment: airless versus HVLP, Wagner Flexio 5000 against Graco TrueCoat 360, judged on 20-year survival. When the data is thin, the review says so and stops there.
